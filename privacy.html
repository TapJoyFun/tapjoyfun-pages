Privacy Policy — TapJoyFun Uploader

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What data this app accesses

TapJoyFun Uploader uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to access the owner's own YouTube channel (TapJoyFun). This includes: uploading videos, reading video and channel information, setting thumbnails and playlists, scheduling publish times, and reading channel analytics (views, watch time, and audience geography).

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos on the TapJoyFun channel. It is not used for any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file. No channel data is sent to any third-party server, and nothing is stored in the cloud by this app.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your data to any third party.

Deleting your data

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account (Security > Third-party access > TapJoyFun), and delete the stored token file (token.json) from the computer. This immediately stops the app from accessing your account.
